# Awesome AI for 3D modeling & Robotics

Public, source-linked examples of general AI models used for 3D modeling, industrial design and CAD, robot control, and animation, collected from X, LinkedIn, YouTube and GitHub. Most cases use OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, which has the largest public record in these tasks; comparisons and benchmark tables also cover Claude, Gemini, Kimi, DeepSeek and vision-language-action policies such as MolmoAct2. Each entry links to the original post, names the author and date, and quotes the author for any number or claim. The demo videos are archived in this repository.

Every link was opened at its original source before the entry was written; media are archived in this repository. Cases are grouped by application, not by model: each case names the model or models in a Model field, and a comparison of several models on one task stays in the case for that task. Where a model identity comes from the author rather than the vendor, the Model field says so. Reposts and commentary are listed under the original case, not as separate entries.

Public demonstrations arrive quickly and from many places, and reviewing each one in full is slow. A public, maintained index is the practical way to keep an assessment both broad and checkable. Within every subsection, posts whose authors published code that runs the demo, or the workflow or evaluation that produced it, come first; then posts with a live demo website; then posts alone. General-purpose tools a demo used, such as Blender MCP, are listed under Agentic tools and integrations and do not count as the demo's code.

## GitHub Projects

Repositories used by the cases, or implementing relevant workflows or evaluations with the models covered here. A tool repository is not necessarily the source of a demonstrated scene.

### Robotics

- [aditya-ramabadran/drivingbench_harness_v1](https://github.com/aditya-ramabadran/drivingbench_harness_v1): DrivingBench's code for supervised, low-speed closed-course driving through comma/openpilot and MCP, with recorded traces. The benchmark compares GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Grok 4.6 and GPT-5.6 Sol; its [downloads](https://drivingbench.com/downloads/) contain recorded artifacts.
- [NVlabs/ENPIRE](https://github.com/NVlabs/ENPIRE): agentic framework for improving real-robot policies through closed-loop physical trials. The harness in the physical in-context-learning demo, linked from the post itself.
- [robocurve/inspect-robots](https://github.com/robocurve/inspect-robots): open-source evals for physical AI. The framework behind Jay Chooi's Astra evaluations ("we evaluate VLAs, WAMs, and LLMs like Astra on robots with Inspect Robots, our open-source framework"). Companions: [inspect-robots-yam](https://github.com/robocurve/inspect-robots-yam) (I2RT YAM bimanual adapters driven by MolmoAct2), [inspect-robots-so101](https://github.com/robocurve/inspect-robots-so101), [worldevals](https://github.com/robocurve/worldevals) (benchmark catalog).

## Related research

- [EPICCAD: editable CAD histories and constraints](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44163-026-01899-5), Zehe Yin, Weixin Lin and Xiangbiao Kong, published 2026-09-02. The authors describe "a multimodal benchmark for history-based CAD modeling". Data and code are "available from the corresponding author on reasonable request." Original proprietary Siemens NX files cannot be redistributed; derived industrial assets have separate sharing conditions. This is research infrastructure, with no openly downloadable release verified, rather than a frontier-model demonstration.
- [Procedura: Agentic 3D Modeling with Procedural Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26238), Youtian Lin and colleagues, arXiv v1 submitted 2026-08-26. The authors describe assemblies whose "named parts are joined by typed, machine-checkable mates." The reported gates are "compile, mate, and connectivity checks". The [project page](https://spatiaos.github.io/projects/procedura/) names "Gemini 3.7 Flash" and "GPT‑5.6‑sol" configurations and reports "simulator‑validated articulation". Results are author-reported; simulator checks do not establish universal manufacturability or physical accuracy. The Code link returns to the project page, and no implementation repository was verified.

## About the Archived Media

Video links open the file from this repository's GitHub Pages site (https://frank-zy-dou.github.io/awesome-ai-3d-modeling-robotics/), which serves them as playable MP4; the same files sit under `assets/videos` in the repository tree. The videos are re-encoded copies of the media attached to the linked posts (H.264, at most 540p; new clips longer than two minutes are stored at 360p). Thumbnails for new clips use the frame at 30 per cent of their duration. Images are resized to at most 1280 px wide. Nothing is cut or edited. Authors who want a file removed can open an issue.
